🔐 Secure Google Gemini Setup Instructions

BrandShield AI is powered exclusively by Google Gemini (gemini-2.5-flash) for multimodal vision understanding, audio ad inspection, and tone analysis:

  1. Open the .env file in the project root.
  2. Add your Gemini API key from Google AI Studio: 
    # Exclusive Multimodal AI Engine
GEMINI_API_KEY=your_gemini_api_key_here
  3. Restart the backend or reload this page. The system will immediately detect Gemini as active.
  4. Rule 10 & 11 Guarantee: If the key is omitted, deterministic checks remain 100% operational, and AI features clearly display their unconfigured state without fake percentages.
