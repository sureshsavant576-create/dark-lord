## Phase 1: Near-Term Milestones (Q1–Q2 2027)

### 1. Creative Studio Ecosystem Integration
- **Figma Real-Time Pre-Flight Plugin**: In-canvas plugin running our deterministic colorimetry and WCAG contrast algorithms inside Figma, alerting designers before artwork export.
- **Adobe Creative Cloud & Express Extension**: Direct connector for Illustrator and Photoshop evaluating artboards against approved Brand DNA centroids.
- **Canva Enterprise App**: In-editor brand guardrails validating brand compliance directly within Canva teams.

### 2. Workflow Automation & Webhooks
- **Slack & Microsoft Teams Bots**: Instant notifications for high-risk claim escalations with in-thread "Approve" or "Request Revision" action buttons.
- **Project Management Webhooks**: Automatic synchronization of review statuses with Jira, Linear, and Asana tasks.

### 3. Multilingual & Regional Brand DNA
- **Regional Dialect Lexicons**: Support for localized terminology, banned idioms, and tone variations across APAC, EMEA, and LATAM markets.
- **Multi-Jurisdiction Regulatory Modules**: Tailored claim verification rules adapted to regional advertising authorities (FTC in the US, ASA in the UK, ASCI in India).

---

## Phase 2: Medium-Term Milestones (Q3–Q4 2027)

### 1. Motion & Video Creative Inspection
- **Frame-by-Frame Video Stream Decoding**: Accelerated GPU decoding evaluating subtitle contrast, on-screen text legibility, logo placement, and disclaimer display durations.
- **Audio Tone & Voiceprint Analysis**: AI verification of voiceover cadence, acoustic brand cues, and sonic branding consistency.

### 2. Cloud-Hosted Generative Inpainting
- **Native Inpainting Integration**: Optional plugin connectors for fine-tuned Stable Diffusion XL and commercial image editing APIs, providing generative content replacement while honoring our deterministic saliency masks.
- **Generative Background Synthesis**: Context-aware replacement of non-compliant background settings with approved brand environments.

### 3. Predictive Guideline Ambiguity Testing
- **Synthetic Creative Stress-Testing**: Automated generation of boundary-testing creatives to evaluate whether newly written brand guidelines create confusion among human reviewers.

---

## Phase 3: Long-Term Horizons (2028+)

### 1. Multi-Brand Enterprise Mesh
- **Holding Company Governance**: Unified parent-company compliance dashboards overseeing dozens of subsidiary brand profiles with cross-brand conflict detection.
- **Co-Branding Partnership Guardrails**: Dual-brand review engine verifying compliance when two distinct brands collaborate on joint marketing assets.

### 2. Automated Regulatory Legal Filings
- **Audit-Ready Evidence Packaging**: Automatic generation of tamper-evident cryptographically signed PDF compliance packs for legal submission during advertising standards audits.
