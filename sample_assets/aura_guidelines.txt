Aura Cosmetics Brand Guidelines (v2.0)
Our brand represents calm, minimalist, clean beauty.
Primary Color: Light Pink (#FFB6C1), Off-White (#FFFFFF), Lavender (#D8BFD8).
Tone: Warm, Mindful, Sophisticated.
Audience: Mindful consumers and beauty enthusiasts.
Strictly Avoid: Loud neon colors, aggressive promotional claims, and buzzwords like 'cheap' or 'miracle guarantee'.
