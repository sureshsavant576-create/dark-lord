APPLE BRAND COMMUNICATION & DESIGN GUIDELINES
Brand Voice: Confident, understated, human, precise, inspiring.
Brand Colors:
- Space Black: #000000
- Pure White: #FFFFFF
- Apple Silver / Gray: #86868B
Visual Aesthetic: Extreme minimalism, generous negative space, crisp focus, architectural lighting.
Strict Avoidances:
- Avoid aggressive retail language, sales gimmicks, and bargain claims.
- Strictly prohibited words: 'super cheap', 'bargain discount', 'miracle deal', 'clearance sale'.
- Avoid loud chaotic graphics, harsh rainbow neon clashing colors, and visual clutter.
